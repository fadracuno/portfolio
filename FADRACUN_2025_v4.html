⚙️ Chapter 2

Reinventing Calibrations

I didn't just run calibration sessions — I transformed the entire methodology from subjective feedback to data-driven, evidence-based learning.

Situation

Calibrations were generic and forgettable

Traditional sessions relied on manual review and general feedback — agents left without clear understanding of why decisions were wrong.

Task

Make calibrations actually drive improvement

Transform sessions into structured learning experiences that reduce recurring errors and build lasting knowledge.

Action

AI-driven, per-agent RCA

Replaced manual review with AI-assisted analysis and ran a specific Root Cause Analysis per agent — maximizing the feedback each one received within our NPT limits, instead of generic group feedback.

Result

10+ sessions. Agents requested more.

Higher learning retention, reduced recurring errors, and — most tellingly — agents started requesting sessions proactively.

SecOps: The Safety Net

720 second opinions across the year. Starting at ~36/week, the need tapered to ~8/week by year-end — a direct sign the team was getting stronger and more self-sufficient.

Situation

Complex cases requiring expert validation

High-stakes decisions where a wrong call could mean missed fraud or false positives — agents needed a trusted second opinion.

Task

Be the team's expert reference

Provide consistent, fast, accurate second opinions without disrupting response times or creating bottlenecks.

Action

From ~36/week down to ~8/week

Heavy expert support early in the year, decreasing steadily as agents internalized the criteria — fewer second opinions needed by December.

Result

720 critical decisions supported

Reduced errors, increased team confidence, and maintained consistency in investigation criteria across the board.
